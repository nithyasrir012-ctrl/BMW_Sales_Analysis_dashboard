# 🚗 BMW Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes BMW sales performance across revenue, profit, units sold, fuel types, dealers, and cities in India.

![BMW Sales Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project transforms raw BMW sales data into a clear, interactive report. It helps stakeholders quickly see which models, cities, dealers, and fuel types drive the most revenue and profit, and how sales change over the year.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Track overall business performance through key KPIs
- Identify the most profitable cities and best-selling models
- Understand cost and revenue distribution by fuel type and dealer
- Analyze monthly sales trends for 2024
- Compare automatic vs. manual transmission orders by model

## 🎛️ Slicers (Filters)

- **Model:** 3 Series, 5 Series, 7 Series, i4, iX, X1, X3, X5
- **City:** Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Delhi, Mumbai
- **Fuel Type:** Diesel, Hybrid, Electric, Petrol

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Pune** is the most profitable city (1.21bn), followed by Bangalore (1.17bn) and Chennai (1.12bn).
- **7 Series** is the top-selling model with 626 units, followed closely by i4 (622).
- Sales **peaked in December (448 units)** and dipped in **August (317 units)**.
- Cost is evenly spread across fuel types, with **Diesel highest at 8.35bn (27.3%)**.
- **Royal Wheels** leads dealer revenue at 22.52%, with all dealers within a close range.
